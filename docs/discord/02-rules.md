## 2. The rules (content)

Order as posted. Each rule says what it covers, an example of what crosses the line, and the usual first response. The response ladder is in `03-moderation.md`.

### 1. Be kind
- Covers: harassment, hate, slurs, threats, personal attacks, pile-ons, sexual content.
- Over the line: insulting a lender over a damage price; mocking someone's English.
- First response: warning. Threats, hate or sexual content: straight to ban (zero-tolerance list in `03-moderation.md`).

### 2. 18 or older
- Covers: the site is 18+, and so is the server (owner decision, 2026-10-20).
- Moderators never ask for ID, birth dates or proof. They act only when someone says or clearly shows they are under 18.
- First response, in this order:
  1. If you will report to Discord, report first, because the message or profile must still exist. If they seem to be under 13, report the account (Report User Profile); Discord's policy is to delete under-13 accounts. If a child seems to be at risk, report it to Discord Trust and Safety and tell the owner (`05-weekly-ops.md` section 5).
  2. Delete any personal details they posted.
  3. Remove them politely (kick, not ban) with the under-18 removal note: not a ban, welcome back at 18.
- If they rejoin while still under 18, ban, and note in `#mod-log` that they can ask for an unban at 18. Log it as "Rule 2 age"; never record an age or birth date.
- An adult who pressures, flirts with, or tries to move a known minor into DMs: zero-tolerance ban, and report to Discord.

### 3. Loans happen on the site, not here
- Covers: arranging a specific loan, handoff, return, damage price or queue spot in Discord, and "who has X I can borrow" or "I'm lending X, DM me" posts.
- Allowed: general talk ("what should I check before borrowing a tent?"), linking to the site's home page, asking how the queue works.
- Not allowed in v1: posting links to individual listings or asking people to request your item. (Owner decision, 07 Q4. Can be revisited later.)
- First response: friendly reminder, point to the site. Repeat: warning.

### 4. No money in Discord, no fee or damage disputes here
- Facts to keep straight (copy-en 20.7 and the Terms): the site charges nothing and takes no payments. A lender may choose to charge a small fee; any fee is agreed and paid directly between the two people, not through the site. The damage price is also settled directly. project-arcade never collects, holds, arranges, guarantees or enforces any payment.
- So in Discord: no asking for or sending money, deposits, tips or payment links; no payment handles; no chasing a debt; no public naming and shaming over a fee or damage dispute.
- Allowed: asking how the damage price works, in general terms.
- Disputes: moderators can't settle them. Point both people to the site's messages, and to the site's Report button if there is abuse or a fake listing.
- First response: delete the message, reminder. Payment requests aimed at other members: treat as a possible scam (rule 8).

### 5. Don't pull people into private chats
- Covers: unsolicited DMs to strike a deal, move a loan or payment off the site, or ask for personal contact details; pressuring anyone in DMs.
- Members may DM friends. The problem is using DMs to get around these rules.
- Tip for members (in the new member guide): if you don't want DMs, open the server menu > Privacy Settings and turn off Direct messages for this server. Discord has no server-wide switch that stops members messaging each other. Moderators only have a 24-hour "Pause DMs" for emergencies (server menu > Security Actions).
- First response: warning. With pressure or money involved: timeout or ban.

### 6. Protect privacy
- Covers: posting anyone's address, phone, email, real name they haven't shared, photos of them, or screenshots of site messages or DMs without consent. Also your own address, phone or ID.
- Introductions: display name and city or area at most.
- First response: delete, then a reminder. Deliberate doxxing: ban.

### 7. No high-risk gear
- Covers: power tools, vehicles (including e-bikes and e-scooters), climbing gear, and medical equipment. They can't be listed on the site in v1, so don't offer, ask for or arrange them here either.
- Also: don't give safety-critical advice on this gear (e.g. "that harness is fine to climb on"). Point people to the manufacturer or a qualified person.
- Allowed: mentioning a hobby in passing.
- First response: reminder. Repeated offers: warning.

### 8. No scams or impersonation
- Covers: phishing links, fake giveaways, payment requests, pretending to be staff or project-arcade, fake "support" DMs.
- Staff will never ask for passwords, payment, or the email you signed up with. (The site never shows emails.)
- First response: ban, delete the messages, post the scam warning (copy-en 20.6) if others may be targeted.

### 9. No ads or self-promotion
- Covers: ads, referral or affiliate links, selling anything, other servers' invite links, spam, repeated messages.
- First response: delete and warn. Spam bots: ban.

### 10. No fundraising and no paid perks
- Covers: any donations, including for project-arcade itself (owner decision, 07 Q8); donation drives, crowdfunding links, paid roles, "boost for perks" offers. Discord has no setting that blocks donation links, so this is enforced by moderators.
- First response: delete and remind.

### 11. Keep it relevant and use the right channel
- Off-topic goes to `#off-topic`. Site questions go to `#help`. Bugs and ideas: Send feedback on the site (a post in `#help` is fine too; tech support will pass it on).
- First response: move or point, no warning.

### 12. Follow moderators
- If a moderator asks you to stop, stop. To disagree, message a moderator or open a private thread in `#contact-mods`. Don't argue in public channels.
- Complaints about a moderator: email [contact email] or message the owner directly, because every moderator can read every ticket.

### Language (owner decision, 07 Q9; part of public rule 11)
- Public wording (copy-en 20.2 rule 11): "Please post in English for now, so our small team of volunteers can follow along."
- Why: AutoMod's built-in lists and the moderators only cover English.
- Moderators: a short phrase in another language is fine, so start with a friendly reminder, never a warning. If a non-English message looks like abuse or a scam, hide or delete it first and check it afterwards (for example with a translation tool).
- Appeals: see `03-moderation.md`.

## 3. Safety guidance for members (new member guide, not rules)

For session 5 to fold into copy-en 20.3 if wanted:
- Meet somewhere public for handoffs when you can, and keep chat in the site's messages.
- Check gear before you use it. The site doesn't inspect or guarantee items.
- Take photos at handoff and at return.
- If you're in danger, contact local emergency services, not us.
- Problems with a listing or a person on the site: use Report on the site. Problems in Discord: `#contact-mods`.

## 4. Staff conventions (not posted as rules)

- No emoji in channel names, role names, forum tags, staff and bot posts, announcements or pinned messages.
- Moderators never DM a member first about a loan, money or a dispute. Moderation DMs are only for warnings, timeout and removal notices, ban notices, scam warnings to someone who was targeted, and replies to reports. Use the copy-en 20.6 templates.
- Staff never ask for a member's email, password, ID or payment details.
- Staff don't moderate cases they are part of (their own loan, a friend, an argument they're in). Hand it to another moderator.
- Staff don't promise outcomes on the site (refunds, account removals, damage payments).
