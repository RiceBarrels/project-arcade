## Boundaries (apply to every file)

1. **Discord is for community talk only.** Borrowing, returning, damage prices and queues all happen on the website (project-arcade.ricebarrel.com). Nobody arranges a specific loan in Discord.
2. **Non-profit, same as the site.** No ads, no paid roles, no server subscriptions, no donations, no boost perks, no sales. (See `07-open-questions.md` Q8.)
3. **18+ only**, same as the site (copy-en section 12). Owner decision, relayed by the coordinator on 2026-10-20.
4. **High-risk categories** (power tools, vehicles, climbing gear, medical equipment) are not offered on the site in v1 and are not arranged or encouraged in Discord.
5. **No emoji** in channel names, role names, forum tags, staff posts, or any text in these docs. Channel names are lowercase English with hyphens.
6. **No secrets.** Never paste bot tokens, webhook URLs, or keys in Discord, chat, or these docs. Invite links are fine.
7. **Owner approval.** Two kinds of change need the owner's plain-text approval in session 8's own terminal:
   - Anything session 8 or the bot/MCP does.
   - Any structural change by anyone: channels, categories, roles, permissions, server settings, bots, integrations, webhooks.

   A relay from another session does not count as approval. Day-to-day moderation and routine posting by human moderators and volunteers follow `03-moderation.md` and the approvals table in `04-who-does-what.md`.
8. **Site reports and Discord reports are separate.** Abuse on the site goes through the site's Report button (copy-en section 9). Bugs and ideas about the site go through Send feedback (copy-en section 19). Discord moderators handle conduct inside Discord.
